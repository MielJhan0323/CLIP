## SFX rules for image pop-ups
- **Professional / good picture** (e.g. an NBA GOAT): **GREEN flash** + cash sfx (no overlay) + ding sfx.
- **Meme picture**: **RED flash** + fah sfx + shocked sfx.
- Also listed: awkward sfx, correct sfx.
- Every image pop-up: background removed if it looks better, **centred and BIG**, with swoosh / ding / fah (meme, bad pick) / money
  (with the **money overlay BEHIND the image**).

## Video (main dopamine effects)
- **Move / cut the video to whoever is talking.**
- If the person is **idle** (e.g. thinking of an NBA GOAT): **slow zoom in + suspense sfx** so it isn't boring.
- **Something changes at least every 3 s** (a cut, a zoom, or a pop-up; not annoying or unnecessary ones).
- Main goal: keep the audience HOOKED, maximise retention.

## Finishing (what human editors do)
- Enhance the video quality with filters: **edge sharpening** (image pop-ups sharpened more), **noise reduction**, **colour + contrast
  enhancement** (match the references), **resolution enhancement** while keeping everything clear.

## Flaw they flagged in our edits
- **Captions linger**: someone says "player", goes silent, and "player" stays on screen. A caption must disappear when the word ends.
